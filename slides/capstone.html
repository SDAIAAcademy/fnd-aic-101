Minutes: opening 15, supervised check 120 (two parts of 60), preparation 45, sixteen defences of 10 with 2 minutes of changeover each (192), review 28, follow-up brief 20. With fewer participants, the review starts earlier.

The supervised check

Two parts of 60 minutes, on paper, without devices

A hand fit

A few rows, a loss, one or two gradient steps or a nearest-neighbour vote.

Reading curves

Training and validation curves: overfit, underfit or a good fit, and what to do next.

Spotting leaks

A data dictionary and a pipeline: which columns and steps leak, and why.

To pass the course. At least 50% on this check, and 60% overall. It counts 30%.

AI-off and devices away: phones in bags, laptops closed. A calculator without connectivity is allowed. Collect the papers at the end of each part. The papers come from the trainer pack, not from the practice bank.

How the capstone is scored

The rubric: five criteria, 100 points

Held-out workflow, fixed seed, test opened once
25
Leakage-safe data handling
15
Threshold from costs and base rate
20
Model card with groups, limits, owner and reviewer
20
Defence: re-run and explain a selected result
20

Full marks on the threshold: costs eight to one, the base rate of the training period, the threshold chosen on validation, the confusion matrix on test at that threshold. Each criterion scores one thing; nothing is counted twice.

The defence

Ten minutes: six of talk, four of questions

1 · The screen

What it flags, for whom, and the costs that set its threshold.

2 · The evidence

Baseline, two models, the leak you removed, the test score opened once.

3 · The card

Rates by sex with counts, limits, and who should not rely on it.

In the questions. Restart the notebook, run all, and explain the result the instructor points to.

Pick the result to explain from the notebook, not from the slides: a number in the confusion matrix, a fold score, the leak check. The repository's history is inspected: a report that grew over several commits.

The follow-up task

The same workflow on a second screen: pressure injury in 90 days

  • About 60 minutes, on your own, within two weeks; tracked, not graded
  • The brief and data dictionary: labs/followup/README.md
  • A second notebook in your capstone repository; send the trainer the commit link
  • One column is recorded after the outcome: find it before you trust a score

The missed-to-needless cost is five to one in this screen. The follow-up shows whether the workflow transfers without the labs around it.
